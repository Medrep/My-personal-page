Hermes Assistant

Hermes is a privately operated AI assistant developed for personal productivity and work organization by Alex Kucheruk.

The application is intended for private use by its owner and is not available for public registration.

What Hermes Does

Hermes helps organize information, analyze correspondence, identify pending actions, prepare for meetings, and retrieve relevant documents.

Google Workspace Integration

With the account owner's explicit authorization, Hermes uses the following Google services:

  • Gmail (read-only): Reading and summarizing emails and conversations, identifying important messages and outstanding tasks.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): Viewing schedules, meetings, attendees, and events to assist with planning.
  • Google Drive (read-only): Searching and reading documents and files relevant to the owner's requests.

Hermes does not require permission to send emails, modify calendar events, or edit Google Drive files for these integrations.

Privacy and Data Processing

Authorized Google data may be processed by configured third-party AI providers to generate summaries, insights, and responses.

Data and analysis results may be stored on a privately managed server. Notifications may be delivered to the owner's authorized Telegram conversation.

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.
