Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. About Hermes

Hermes Assistant is a privately operated AI assistant maintained by Alex Kucheruk for personal productivity and work organization.

Hermes is intended for use by its owner and does not offer public registration.

2. Google Data Access

With the account owner's authorization, Hermes may access the following Google services:

  • Gmail: Read email messages, headers, conversation threads, and associated metadata to summarize correspondence and identify important messages and pending actions.
  • Google Calendar: Read calendar events, schedules, descriptions, and attendees to assist with planning and meeting preparation.
  • Google Drive: Search and read files and document contents relevant to the owner's requests.

These integrations use read-only Google OAuth permissions. Hermes does not require permission to send email, change calendar events, or modify Google Drive files for these functions.

3. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through Google APIs is used to provide requested assistant functionality, including summaries, search, meeting preparation, reminders, and identification of outstanding tasks.

Relevant information from different authorized sources may be combined to support these functions.

4. AI Processing and Third Parties

Hermes uses configured third-party AI inference providers to analyze information and generate responses. Relevant portions of authorized Google data may be transmitted to these providers when necessary to perform an assistant task.

Notifications and summaries may also be delivered through Telegram to the owner's authorized private conversation.

Google user data is not sold, shared with advertising networks, or used for advertising targeting.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Hermes runs on a privately managed server. OAuth credentials, message identifiers, processing records, summaries, conversation history, and selected information may be stored to provide assistant functionality and continuity.

Data is retained for as long as it is needed for these purposes or until removed by the operator. Backup copies may remain until their normal replacement or deletion.

Connected Google account information is not published on this website.

6. Security

Access to Hermes is restricted to authorized use. The system uses account authorization, access controls, and private server administration to protect stored information.

7. Revocation and Deletion

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Revoking authorization prevents further access through the revoked credentials but does not automatically erase previously stored records.

Requests regarding access to or deletion of stored information can be sent to the operator.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes uses information received from Google APIs to provide the user-facing assistant features described in this policy, subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is not used for advertising or sold to data brokers.

Google Workspace data is used solely to provide the requested user-facing assistant functionality. It is not used to develop, train, or improve generalized or non-personalized AI or machine learning models. The use of information received from Google Workspace APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
